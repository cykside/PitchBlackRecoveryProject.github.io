Fastboot Install (Recovery Partition)

1. Enter Bootloader:

adb reboot bootloader

2. Flash Recovery:

{{ fastboot }} flash recovery pbrp.img

Tip: Reboot to recovery immediately after flashing to prevent stock ROM from overwriting PBRP.
