Odin Install

For Samsung devices. Requires Odin on PC.

  1. Power off device. Boot into Download Mode.
  2. Connect to PC via USB.
  3. Open Odin. Ensure device is detected (Blue COM port).
  4. Click AP (or PDA) button.
  5. Select the PBRP-*.tar.md5 file.
  6. Uncheck "Auto Reboot" in options.
  7. Click Start.
  8. Once finished, manually force reboot into Recovery (Vol Up + Power + Home/Bixby).
